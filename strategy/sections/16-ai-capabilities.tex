\strategyPart{16 \textbar\ AI Capabilities}{AI-Enabled Organizational Capabilities}

AI empowers a company by strengthening ten organizational capabilities, and
only when seven supporting conditions are in place.

\section{The ten capabilities}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Faster decision-making} — less time to find, analyze, compare
        and recommend.
  \item \textbf{Better decision quality} — patterns humans miss across large
        datasets, given good data and transparency.
  \item \textbf{Increased productivity} — less repetitive work; more time for
        creativity, negotiation, problem-solving and strategy.
  \item \textbf{Lower operating costs} — fewer manual steps, errors, rework
        and downtimes.
  \item \textbf{Improved customer experience} — faster, personalized,
        proactive and 24-hour service.
  \item \textbf{Greater scalability} — more customers, transactions and data
        without proportional headcount.
  \item \textbf{Faster innovation} — research, experiments, design and
        prototyping accelerate.
  \item \textbf{Better organizational memory} — decisions and lessons remain
        searchable.
  \item \textbf{More resilient operations} — early warnings for supply,
        cyber, equipment and demand risk.
  \item \textbf{New revenue opportunities} — AI-powered products, services
        and intelligent subscriptions.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conditions required}

\textbf{Clear business purpose, good data, process redesign, employee
adoption, AI governance, technology integration and continuous measurement.}
Usage alone does not demonstrate value — the company must measure both AI
system performance and business outcomes [NIST AI RMF; McKinsey 2025].

\section{Final categorization}

AI in companies serves \textbf{seven purposes}: employee productivity,
customer value, operational efficiency, decision intelligence, innovation,
organizational coordination and business transformation. The WEF framework
locates these within five transformation areas — customer experience,
operations, R\&D, strategy and talent [WEF--Accenture 2026] — and its agentic
research predicts end-to-end work redesign as enterprises rebuild for
autonomous agents [WEF 2025].

\takeaway{AI strengthens ten organizational capabilities only when purpose, data, redesign, adoption, governance and measurement are in place.}
