\strategyPart{Appendix \textbar\ Recommended Titles}{Additional Chapters Recommended for This Whitepaper}

The following titles are candidates for the next expansion of this document.
Each is grounded in the research base of this survey.

\section{Recommended additional chapters}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{The Economics of AI Transformation} — ROI by sector, payback
        periods, TCO and capex/opex allocation [McKinsey 2023; Deloitte].
  \item \textbf{Sector Deep Dives} — banking, retail, healthcare, manufacturing
        and public sector [Stanford AI Index; WEF].
  \item \textbf{AI Product Management Playbook} — discovery, prioritization,
        human--AI workflows, model and agent requirements [product-management
        literature].
  \item \textbf{Agentic AI: From Copilots to Autonomous Workforces} — autonomy
        levels, orchestration, permissions, escalation [Gartner 2024; MCP].
  \item \textbf{Responsible AI Metrics that Boards Can Read} — fairness,
        safety, privacy and audit KPIs [NIST AI RMF; OECD].
  \item \textbf{The AI-Native Startup Playbook} — building from day one with
        AI at the core [Stanford AI Index; venture research].
  \item \textbf{Workforce of the Future: Jobs, Skills and Reskilling at Scale}
        — labor-market data and training economics [WEF Future of Jobs; OECD].
  \item \textbf{Measuring Organizational Culture Change} — culture surveys,
        adoption cohorts and behavioral telemetry [HBR; Microsoft WTI].
  \item \textbf{AI Transformation in the EU: Regulation as Strategy} — EU AI
        Act, GDPR, ISO/IEC 42001 as competitive levers [European Commission].
  \item \textbf{A Quantitative Maturity Index} — a composite AI transformation
        score combining the stocks, ladder and operating-model assessments.
\end{enumerate}
