\proposalpart{04 \textbar\ Methodology}{How We Work}

Our methodology is built for speed and certainty. Every project follows four
phases, and each phase ends with a tangible checkpoint.

\section{The four phases}
\begin{center}
  \begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.45\linewidth}@{\hspace{0.8cm}}p{0.45\linewidth}@{}}
    \infobox{\textbf{1. Discover}}{%
      Understand your goals, data and constraints; define the success metrics.%
    } &
    \infobox{\textbf{2. Design}}{%
      Architect the solution and prepare the data pipeline.%
    } \\[0.6em]
    \infobox{\textbf{3. Build}}{%
      Develop, train and rigorously evaluate the model.%
    } &
    \infobox{\textbf{4. Deliver}}{%
      Deploy, monitor and improve continuously with your team.%
    } \\
  \end{tabular}
\end{center}

\section{Why our approach works}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Short cycles} — a working result in weeks, not quarters.
  \item \textbf{Deployment first} — the target environment shapes the design.
  \item \textbf{Measured impact} — every phase closes against agreed KPIs.
\end{itemize}
