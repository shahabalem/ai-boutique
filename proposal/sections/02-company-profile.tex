\proposalpart{02 \textbar\ Company Profile}{About AI Boutique}

AI Boutique is a specialist consultancy focused on the design and delivery of
artificial intelligence solutions for businesses of every size. We are a small,
senior team — which means you work directly with the people building your
solutions, not through a sales layer.

\section{What sets us apart}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Senior, hands-on team} — no hand-offs between consulting and delivery.
  \item \textbf{Business-first design} — every solution is tied to a measurable KPI.
  \item \textbf{Production focus} — models that run in your environment, not demos.
  \item \textbf{Knowledge transfer} — your team learns to own and evolve the solution.
\end{itemize}
